TV & Film Extras Membership Agreement

By applying to join Villains Are Us TV & Film Extras, you confirm that the information you provide is accurate and that you have permission to submit the headshot supplied.

Membership costs £11.99 for 12 months. Membership allows Villains Are Us to keep your submitted details and headshot on its TV & Film Extras database and consider you for suitable background and supporting opportunities.

Membership does not guarantee employment, bookings, auditions, a minimum amount of work or selection for any production. Opportunities depend on production requirements, casting criteria, suitability, location and your availability.

You consent to Villains Are Us storing and processing your submitted details and headshot for extras administration, casting consideration, availability requests, submissions to legitimate casting or production professionals where relevant, and booking administration.

When suitable opportunities arise, Villains Are Us may contact you by email with availability requests, filming dates, locations, call times, wardrobe or appearance requirements, available payment information and production instructions. An availability request is not a confirmed booking.

If booked, you agree to follow reasonable production instructions, behave professionally and comply with relevant confidentiality, safety, appearance, wardrobe and conduct requirements.

You may request correction or deletion of your profile by contacting info@villiansareus.co.uk, subject to lawful retention requirements. Nothing in this agreement affects your statutory rights under UK law.
