01 · OUR STORY

It started with a belief in distinctive talent.

The characters audiences remember are rarely forgettable — and neither are the actors who create them.

Villains Are Us was created to represent professional actors and performers with individuality, discipline and screen presence. We are especially drawn to talent with character: faces, voices and performances that feel authentic, specific and impossible to ignore.

Our approach is personal and focused. We aim to understand each performer as an individual, champion the work they do best and present them professionally for opportunities across film, television, theatre and commercial production.

WE ARE NOT INTERESTED IN BLENDING IN.

We champion performers who change the temperature when they enter a scene.
